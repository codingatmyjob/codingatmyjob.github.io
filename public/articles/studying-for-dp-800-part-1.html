Why DP-800?

I've been looking for a certification that bridges the gap between these stat-padding entry and foundational level certs, and something more robust, practical, and aligned with my current work role. Microsoft DP-800, SQL AI Developer Associate, checks those boxes. It's deeper knowledge-wise than an entry-level cert like AI-900, and also requires the practical application knowledge, and fluency working in Microsoft's products to pass the interactive simulations.

The goal of this Series

This post is the starting point for a longer series. Instead of publishing one long review after I pass (which I will probably still do), I want to document what I studied, what felt confusing, what resources I used, and really drill down into the topics that I turned into mini-projects. That should make the final writeup better, but more importantly it should give me a cleaner record of how I prepared.

The certification is broad enough that I can split it into multiple topic-driven articles. I'm specifically looking at AI integration patterns, native vector engineering, and AI performance optimization as potential articles.

What the Next Posts Will Cover

The plan is to analyze and split up the objectives into two groups: topics that can be learned mostly from reading, and topics that could benefit from hands-on labs. I'll then try and group labs into similar topics for easy article writing. The overarching goal is to document the learning process, and also share the resources that helped me.

If I do this correctly, the final article titled "how I passed DP-800" post should be the least interesting article from the series. The value will be in those topic driven project articles that show practical application of specific concepts along the way.
